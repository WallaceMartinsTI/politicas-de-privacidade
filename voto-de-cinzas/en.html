Privacy Policy — Vow of Ashes (Voto de Cinzas)

Last updated: 2026-10-26

Developer: Wallace Coimbra dos Santos Martins

Contact: wallace159santos@hotmail.com

This policy explains what data the Voto de Cinzas app ("the game") uses and how. In short: the game needs no sign-up, does not collect your name or email, and keeps your progress on your own device. The only data handled by third parties comes from ads, purchases, and usage and crash measurement (Firebase), described below.

1. Data that stays on your device

  • Game progress (characters, items, gold, gems, achievements, settings, language) is saved locally on the device. We do not send it to any server of ours, because we have none.
  • Android backup: if Android's automatic backup is on, the system may copy your progress to your Google account. That is controlled by Android and your Google account, not by us.
  • Export/import: the game can generate a code or file of your progress. You decide who keeps or shares it.

2. Ads

The game uses Google AdMob to show ads (for example rewarded ads that grant gems when watched, and ads after finishing a chapter). To show them, AdMob/Google may collect and use on your device: advertising ID, IP address, device and system information, and ad interaction data, including to personalize ads, measure results and prevent fraud.

  • On first launch, and whenever the law requires, the game shows a consent screen (Google UMP). You can refuse personalized ads and change your choice later in the game's settings.
  • You can also reset or delete your advertising ID and limit personalization in Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
  • More information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads and https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. Usage measurement and crash reports (Firebase)

The game uses Google Firebase (Analytics and Crashlytics) to learn, in aggregate, how the game is used and where it fails, so we can improve it. We collect game events (for example: battle started, boss won or lost, chapter completed, ending chosen, ad shown, "Remove ads" purchase), an installation identifier generated by Firebase, approximate country (derived from IP), device and system data (model, Android version, language) and, on a crash, the technical error report.

  • We do not collect your name, email or anything that identifies you, and we do not link this data to an account, because the game has no accounts.
  • Measurement is only turned on after your consent, in regions where the law requires it, and you can change your choice in Settings → Privacy options. Where no choice is required, it is on by default.
  • More information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

4. In-app purchases

Purchases (such as "Remove ads" and gem packs, when available) are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store payment details; we only receive confirmation that the purchase happened.

5. What we do NOT collect

Name, email, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or any account.

6. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data. If you are a guardian and believe a child provided data, contact us at the address above.

7. Your rights

We do not keep personal data that identifies you. For data handled by Google (ads, measurement and crashes, backup, purchases), use your Google account tools and your device's privacy options (for example, resetting the advertising ID). For questions or requests, write to wallace159santos@hotmail.com.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this text. The date at the top shows the version in force, and relevant changes will be announced in the game or on the store page.
